Hi, I’m Liwen!👋

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Developmental Psychology at the National University of Singapore (NUS), expected to graduate in 2027. I conduct my doctoral research in the NUS Child Development Lab under the supervision of Dr. Xiao Pan Ding.

Prior to this, I earned a M.Soc.Sci. (Research) in Psychology from NUS and a B.S. in Psychology from East China Normal University.

My research focuses on moral development, particularly the role of the self-system in children’s moral behavior. See my Research for more details.

I am currently seeking academic positions and postdoctoral opportunities. Please feel free to contact me at yuliwen@u.nus.edu!
